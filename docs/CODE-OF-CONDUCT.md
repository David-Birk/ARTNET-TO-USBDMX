# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, discussions and all other contact about this project.

## Please

- Be friendly and respectful.
- Stay on topic.
- Give helpful feedback.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience.

## Not allowed

- Insults, harassment or discrimination
- Threats
- Spam
- Sharing private information about other people

## Reporting

Send an email to PigcraftTV50@gmail.com. Reports are treated confidentially.

Comments that break these rules can be removed, and people who repeatedly break them can be blocked.
